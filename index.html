Help shoppers find the right product and complete their purchase with AI voice and chat. Turn browsers into buyers on autopilot.

Ringg AI Shopping Assistant is an AI agent that talks to your shoppers the way your best store associate would. It answers product questions, compares options, suggests the right size and fit, recommends bundles and add-ons, and sends a personalised checkout link by SMS or WhatsApp when the shopper is ready to buy. It speaks 20+ languages, works across your storefront chat, inbound calls and outbound callbacks, and runs 24/7 without code or a call center. Every conversation includes recordings, transcripts and attributed revenue, so your team can see exactly which conversations drove sales.

  • Guide shoppers to the right product with AI voice and chat conversations
  • Recommend sizes, bundles and add-ons to grow average order value
  • Send personalised product and checkout links by SMS or WhatsApp
  • Call back shoppers for price drops and back-in-stock alerts in 20+ languages
  • Review recordings, transcripts and revenue influenced by every conversation
